About Project Bayaw

PRC ABE Board Exam Reviewer

A free reviewer for the Philippine Agricultural & Biosystems Engineering licensure exam, covering all three areas of the PRC Table of Specifications with spaced-repetition study, worked solutions and timed mock exams.

Where the questions come from

Recalled board exam items, preboard and mock exams, published ABE reviewers (including Belonio's Problems & Solutions), and the Philippine Agricultural Engineering Standards (PAES).

How answers are checked

Answer keys were reviewed against the standards and source solutions, and every concept question explains why each choice is right or wrong. Found a mistake? Tap Report on the question and it goes straight to the owner.

Your data

Your progress is saved on this device and synced to your account so you can continue on another phone or PC.

Built by Bryan Lester B. Tapec, RABE. Meet the creator for partnerships with review centers and schools.

Independent study tool. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC).
