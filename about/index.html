01 · ABOUT

Engineer by license, builder by habit.

I'm Bryan, a Registered Agricultural and Biosystems Engineer who knows how heavy the board exam feels, and I built the reviewer I wish I had.

Project Bayaw App started as a way to make serious ABE review easier to reach: real board-style questions, worked solutions, mock exams that feel like the PRC, and a study plan that remembers what you keep missing. I graduated from Cagayan State University – Sanchez Mira and passed the ABE licensure exam the same year. Since then I've been building new habits and learning whatever pulls my interest, and Project Bayaw App is where all of it comes together. I build and run it myself, from writing the questions to shipping the site.
